AI RESEARCH

The Research Behind the Products

A lot of my work starts with a question: does this actually work for the person using it?

With AI, that question gets complicated. A response can sound convincing without being accurate or useful. People can interpret the same system differently depending on their experiences, expectations, and the situation they're using it in.

I've explored these questions from a few angles, including how AI practitioners evaluate fairness, how people from different communities experience AI assistants, and how design choices influence trust and reliance. Lately, I've been really interested in what these findings mean for the people building and evaluating AI products!
