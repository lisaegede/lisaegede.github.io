she/her · human researcher

I'm a mixed-methods researcher completing my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University's Human-Computer Interaction Institute.

Much of my research is motivated by questions around what it means for AI systems to work well for diverse communities. I study how people seek information and make decisions with AI in high-stakes contexts, and how the social and human-like qualities of these systems influence their experiences. My research has informed both product design decisions and evaluation practices for generative AI systems.

Beyond my work at CMU, I've spent time at Microsoft Research, where my work has focused on responsible AI and the evaluation of anthropomorphic behaviors in generative AI, and at IBM Research, where I developed a water availability prediction tool to support resource planning in Kenya.

Before grad school, I studied Computer Science at the University of Oklahoma, where I conducted privacy and security research in the OU Data Analytics Lab. I also spent some of my time outside the lab helping organize Oklahoma's first student-run hackathon, Hacklahoma!

My non-academic pursuits include curating Spotify playlists and documenting life on film!*~
